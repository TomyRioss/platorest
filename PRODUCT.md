# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Restaurant staff (waiters, cashiers, kitchen) on tablets mid-shift — need speed, high contrast, large touch targets, works in bright/glare-heavy or dim kitchen lighting. Restaurant owners/admins managing menu, stock, loyalty, sales from desktop — need clear visibility and control without complexity. Marketing pages (funcionalidades, testimonios) target prospective owners evaluating the product; secondary to the app itself.

## Product Purpose
Restaurant management platform: menu, checkout/POS, kitchen display, stock, loyalty, admin. Success = owners get clear visibility and control over their operation (stock, sales, loyalty) without the tool getting in the way, while staff-facing screens (POS/KDS) stay fast and error-free under service pressure.

## Brand Personality
Fast, efficient, no-nonsense. Built for speed under pressure on the floor; professional and trustworthy on the admin side. Not playful, not decorative — utilitarian confidence.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS/cream-template look (cream/sand body backgrounds, gradient text, tiny uppercase eyebrows, numbered section scaffolding). Avoid dated/clunky legacy POS software aesthetics.

## Design Principles
- Speed over decoration: every screen optimized for the fastest correct action, not visual flourish.
- Legible under pressure: high contrast, large touch targets, readable in bright or dim kitchen/floor lighting.
- Owner clarity: stock/sales/loyalty data surfaced plainly, no dashboards that require interpretation.
- Trustworthy, not cute: professional tone for an owner's livelihood tool — no gimmicks.
- One system, two speeds: shared design tokens/components across fast operational screens and calmer admin screens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA minimum. Tablet/glare-heavy environment support: high contrast ratios beyond AA minimums where feasible, large touch targets (≥44px), legible in direct sunlight and dim kitchen conditions.
